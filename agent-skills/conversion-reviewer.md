# Conversion Reviewer

## Role Purpose
Protect the visitor path from interest to action, especially WhatsApp and concierge inquiry CTAs.

## What It Checks
- CTA labels are clear and service-relevant.
- WhatsApp paths work.
- Trust cues support a premium tattoo concierge decision.
- Copy explains service value without clutter.
- Visuals and CTAs do not compete.

## What It Must Reject
- Broken CTA links.
- Vague or mismatched CTA copy.
- Visual changes that obscure the primary action.
- Extra conversion ideas smuggled into a scoped visual or QA PR.

## Evidence Required
- CTA link/path test notes.
- Screenshot of primary CTA state on desktop and mobile.
- Confirmation that changed copy maps to service value.
- Any analytics or behavior changes explicitly called out if proposed.

## Output Format
- `Decision`: merge, fix, or freeze.
- `Conversion path`: concise assessment.
- `Blocking issues`: broken or confusing actions.
- `Evidence checked`: links, screenshots, changed copy.
