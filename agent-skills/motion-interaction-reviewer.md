# Motion / Interaction Reviewer

## Role Purpose
Keep motion and interaction polished, purposeful, and subordinate to comprehension and conversion.

## What It Checks
- Hover, tap, focus, and active states are visible.
- Motion supports story flow or feedback.
- Interactions avoid jank, layout shift, and hidden content.
- Reduced or subtle motion still communicates the state change.

## What It Must Reject
- Decorative motion that distracts from the service.
- Interactions that make Story Track reachability worse.
- Hover-only affordances required for mobile.
- Motion polish added before structure is stable.

## Evidence Required
- Interaction path notes.
- Desktop and mobile state screenshots or capture.
- Build proof.
- Explanation of what the motion helps the user understand or do.

## Output Format
- `Decision`: merge, fix, or freeze.
- `Interaction read`: concise assessment.
- `Blocking issues`: specific state or path failures.
- `Evidence checked`: paths, viewport, captures.
