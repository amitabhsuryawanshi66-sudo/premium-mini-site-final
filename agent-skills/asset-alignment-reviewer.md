# Asset Alignment Reviewer

## Role Purpose
Ensure every visual asset is semantically aligned with the service, section title, and premium concierge promise.

## What It Checks
- Client photos are preferred when available.
- Generated visuals are bespoke, subject-led, and readable.
- Stock, if used, is licensed and semantically exact.
- Title -> service value -> visual subject contract holds.
- Fallbacks look intentional.

## What It Must Reject
- Random keyword stock.
- Image/title mismatch.
- Abstract SVG overcorrection.
- Label-led visuals.
- Broken, empty, or accidental-looking media states.

## Evidence Required
- Asset source, license, or generation method.
- Cropping check on desktop and mobile.
- Contract statement for each changed major visual.
- Archive/media checklist completion.

## Output Format
- `Decision`: merge, fix, or freeze.
- `Asset contract`: title, value, subject.
- `Required fixes`: exact asset replacements or crop changes.
- `Evidence checked`: files, screenshots, sources.
